Famous Places in Vietnam

Hanoi

Hanoi is the capital of Vietnam and is famous for its history and traditional culture.

Ha Long Bay

Ha Long Bay is famous for its beautiful limestone islands and blue water.

Da Nang

Da Nang is a modern coastal city with beautiful beaches and tourist destinations.

Hoi An

Hoi An is famous for its old town, colorful lanterns and traditional architecture.

Vietnamese Countryside

The Vietnamese countryside has beautiful rice fields, villages and peaceful landscapes.
